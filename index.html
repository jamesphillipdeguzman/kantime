Choir Member Quick Guide

Master your rehearsal routine, background tracking, and choir standings in 5 simple steps.

Step 1
Set Up Profile & Avatar

Select your choir voice section (Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass, or Primary), enter your name, and choose an illustrated choir portrait to represent you on the community boards.

Step 2
Configure Preferences

Adjust your rehearsal timer block anywhere from 5 to 60 minutes (15 min default) and personalize your target Stake Conference or event date to stay focused.

Step 3
Practice & Background Timer

Choose your anthem from the dropdown and tap Start Session. Click "🎼 Interactive Sheet & Audio" or "▶️ Watch Video"—the countdown timer continues accurately in the background while you sing along!

Step 4
Inactivity Auto-Pause

KanTime features automatic idle detection. If no interaction (mouse, touch, or keys) occurs for 5 minutes during an active session, the timer pauses with an "Are you still practicing?" modal to keep recorded time honest.

Step 5
Leaderboards & Standings

When the countdown reaches 00:00, your rehearsal minutes are submitted to the Stake Choir Sheet, instantly updating the Top 10 Dedicated Singers and the Section Standings.
